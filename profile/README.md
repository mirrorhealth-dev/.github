## About

MirrorHealth is the infrastructure that securely activates and standardizes clinical data. We mediate between healthcare providers and researchers to generate real-world and in-silico evidence without compromising privacy.

Using generative AI and automated semantic standardization, we turn fragmented clinical records into actionable insights, without data ever leaving its source infrastructure.

Private by design. Aligned with EHDS and GDPR.

## Solutions

| Segment | What we offer |
|---|---|
| **Pharma** | Simulation of patient cohorts, synthetic control arms and clinical trajectories before, during and after clinical trials. |
| **Hospitals** | Conversion of heterogeneous data and unstructured clinical notes into interoperable, research-ready OMOP-CDM models through federated networks. |
| **Medical AI** | Synthetic populations of high biological fidelity to train, fine-tune and validate models, free of re-identification risk. |

## Technology

- **NLP structuring:** extraction of clinical entities from free-text medical notes.
- **Semantic mapping:** grounding of variables to the OMOP-CDM standard using vector embeddings.
- **Declarative standardization:** reproducible mappings defined with LinkML and BAML, with human-in-the-loop validation.
- **Federated simulation:** multi-center in-silico queries with no row-level data transfer.
